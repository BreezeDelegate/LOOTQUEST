# **App Name**: LootQuest

## Core Features:

- Interactive Narrative Journey: Users embark on a 'fictive journey' through an interactive storyline, making choices that affect their path and discoveries.
- Digital Loot Discovery: Based on narrative choices and progress, users discover and 'loot' unique digital artifacts or achievements within the app.
- AI-Powered Loot Lore: Utilize a generative AI tool to create rich, unique descriptive lore and backstory for each discovered digital 'loot' item, enhancing its fictional depth.
- Persistent Loot Inventory: A dedicated section to view, categorize, and admire all collected digital loot and artifacts.
- Journey & Loot Saving: Save user progress through the narrative journey and all collected loot items for continued engagement across sessions.
- Social Sharing: Enable users to easily share their favorite collected loot items or journey milestones with others.

## Style Guidelines:

- The concept of a 'fictive journey' and 'loot discovery' suggests an immersive, slightly mysterious adventure. A dark color scheme will enhance this feeling, providing an intimate backdrop for the narrative and discovered treasures.
- Primary color: A deep, rich indigo blue (#3D3D8F) that evokes mystery, depth, and the unknown of a fantastical journey, yet feels grounded and inviting.
- Background color: A very dark, subtle blue-gray (#1E1E2E) serving as a hushed canvas, allowing the interactive elements and collected loot to stand out with prominence.
- Accent color: A vibrant, clear sky blue (#5299E0) to highlight important actions, interactive choices, or moments of discovery, creating a focal point amidst the deeper tones.
- Headlines: 'Alegreya' (serif), chosen for its elegant and intellectual feel, fitting the narrative depth of a fictional journey and giving titles a sense of significance.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif), providing clear, modern, and highly readable text for the story progression and loot descriptions, ensuring an enjoyable reading experience without distraction.
- Utilize icons that reflect themes of adventure, fantasy, and discovery, such as stylized maps, treasure chests, mystical glyphs, or antique artifact representations.
- Implement subtle yet engaging animations for narrative transitions and the act of 'looting' an item. For instance, a glowing effect when an item is acquired or a smooth dissolve between story scenes.
